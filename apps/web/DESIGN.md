# Hashi design system

Every visual property in the app resolves to a token below. The live specimen of all of it is `/dev`.

## Direction

A bridge for dollars between chains and Bitcoin Cash: "vermilion bridge". Cream paper, black ink and one vermilion, with candy colours for chains and states.

## Typography

- **Figtree** 400/500/600/700 for all words. Display: 500, −4% tracking, line-height 0.97 (the display face). Titles: 500, −4%, 1.1.
- **Geist Mono** 400/500 for amounts (tabular figures), addresses, transaction ids and labels. Labels: 11px, +8% tracking, uppercase, 62% ink ("/ BEST RETURN", "01 — PAY").

## Shape and depth

- Radius base 14px (controls); chips 12px; cards ~25px; large panels ~36px; pills fully round.
- Shadows: the glossy button (layered white inner highlights + a vermilion drop), `shadow-card`, `shadow-float`.
- The glow: a vermilion-to-amber bloom, blurred 60px, wider than the object it sits behind.

## Motion

- Headlines sharpen in per word (Motion-Primitives `text-effect`, blur preset).
- Amounts roll (NumberFlow). Route highlight glides (Motion-Primitives `animated-background`, spring 0.4 s). Best route wears a vermilion beam (Magic UI `border-beam`).
- Tracker: done segments fill green; the active step pulses vermilion. Live events slide in (Magic UI `animated-list`).
- Theme switches spread from the toggle in a circle (Magic UI `animated-theme-toggler`).
- Everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Banned

- Stock shadcn greys, Inter or Geist as the brand face, purple-to-pink defaults.
- Letters or dots in place of real logos.
- Sample people, emails or invented numbers in the product (specimens on `/dev` are labelled as such).
