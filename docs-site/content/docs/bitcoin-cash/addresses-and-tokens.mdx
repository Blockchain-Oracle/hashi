A Bitcoin Cash address has two spellings from the same key:

| Form | Starts with | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| plain | `bitcoincash:q…` | BCH. Bridges pay here, or a swap's contract on the way to PUSD and MUSD (NEAR Intents does not accept the token form) |
| token-aware | `bitcoincash:z…` | CashTokens such as PUSD and MUSD. Cauldron sends bought tokens here |

Your wallet holds both; Hashi shows the right one in the review: **To** is the plain form for BCH and the token form for PUSD or MUSD.

## Tokens carry a little BCH

Every token output on Bitcoin Cash carries some BCH with it. A Cauldron buy gives your PUSD or MUSD output 1,000 sats, which stay yours; they are not a fee.

## Sending to another address

Plain BCH into Bitcoin Cash can go to any Bitcoin Cash address you paste in settings. Hashi checks it: mainnet only (`bitcoincash:`), no mixed case, a valid checksum, not an EVM address. PUSD and MUSD always land in your connected wallet, because the swap contract pays only your connected wallet's token address.
