Bitcoin Cash has dollars of its own: **PUSD** (ParyonUSD) and **MUSD** (MoriaUSD), issued as CashTokens and traded on Cauldron. Most of the world's dollars live elsewhere, as USDC and USDT. Hashi is the bridge between them, built from Bitcoin Cash's own tools:

## What Hashi does on Bitcoin Cash

- Reads your wallet's coins, tokens included, through Electrum servers.
- Asks Cauldron's Router to build each trade from your own coins, or from a swap contract's coin.
- Asks your wallet to sign over WizardConnect (or signs a swap contract's trade with that swap's own key), then broadcasts it.
- Builds the plain payments a bridge needs, to its rules (THORChain's memo included).

Every transaction from your wallet is approved in your wallet. The only other key is each swap contract's own trade key, made in your browser and kept there; Hashi's server holds none.
