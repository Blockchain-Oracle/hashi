ParyonUSD sponsors BCH BLAZE 2026, and PUSD is the token Hashi starts on.

## Through Hashi

- **In**: USDC or USDT on eight chains → PUSD or MUSD. A bridge delivers BCH; one Cauldron trade turns it into the token.
- **Out**: PUSD or MUSD → USDC or USDT on eight chains. One Cauldron sale that pays NEAR Intents directly (one approval); through THORChain, the sale and then the BCH with its memo.
- **On Bitcoin Cash**: PUSD ⇄ MUSD ⇄ BCH in one Cauldron trade.

Their dollar values in Hashi come from Cauldron's own pre-trade price for each token, times the live BCH price: never an assumed $1.
