## Activity

**Your swaps** lists every swap started in this browser, newest first: "10 USDC → ≈ 9.94 PUSD", the chains, the route, how long ago, and its status. Filters with counts: **All**, **In progress**, **Completed**, **Refunded, failed or expired**. Swaps still on their way are re-checked every 20 seconds while the page is open, and the **Activity** link in the bar shows how many are in progress.

## Closing the tab

Each step is saved in your browser (IndexedDB) before the next one starts. When you come back:

| Where the swap was | What happens |
|---|---|
| bridging | it picks up on its own and keeps checking every 5 seconds |
| waiting for your deposit | it waits for your click: **Approve the deposit in your … wallet** |
| PUSD or MUSD sold through THORChain, the BCH not sent yet | **Send the BCH to THORChain**: the bridge is quoted then, for exactly the BCH the sale produced |
| BCH at the swap contract, PUSD or MUSD to come | Hashi makes the trade with no approval while the tracker is open; if the BCH went back to your wallet, **Swap the BCH into PUSD** (one approval) |
| its quote ran out before your first approval | it ends as **Expired**, with nothing sent |

On the swap page, a banner points at the swap that needs you: "Your BCH arrived. Finish: swap it into PUSD", "PUSD sold: send the BCH on", "A swap is waiting for its deposit", or "A swap is on its way".

<Callout title="Selling PUSD or MUSD back to USDC or USDT">
Through NEAR Intents it is one approval: the Cauldron sale pays NEAR's deposit address in the same transaction, so there is nothing in between. Through THORChain it asks your Bitcoin Cash wallet twice: sell on Cauldron, then send the BCH with THORChain's memo. The sale is saved the moment it is out, so stopping between the two is safe: the swap reopens at the send, and THORChain is quoted then for the BCH the sale produced.
</Callout>

## Quotes that run out

A swap that has moved nothing ends as **Expired** once its quote runs out: NEAR Intents' deposit deadline, THORChain's quote expiry, or an hour for a swap that starts with a Cauldron trade. Hashi never pays into an expired quote. Before ending a NEAR Intents swap it asks NEAR whether a deposit arrived after all (say, the tab closed just after you approved), and follows that deposit instead.

Swaps live only in the browser that started them; there is no account. The server keeps only each swap contract's public terms, so it can send unfinished BCH back to you.
