Hashi (橋, *bridge*) moves dollars between the big chains and Bitcoin Cash. Bring **USDC or USDT** from Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche, BNB Chain or Solana, and receive **PUSD**, **MUSD** or plain **BCH** in your own Bitcoin Cash wallet. Or go the other way. Or swap PUSD, MUSD and BCH with each other on Bitcoin Cash alone.

Neither NEAR Intents nor THORChain lists PUSD or MUSD. Hashi joins those two, which move plain BCH, (NEAR Intents and THORChain) to Cauldron, the exchange where PUSD and MUSD trade, and prices the whole trip as one route.

## How it works

<Diagram name="system" alt="Hashi's system: the swap screen, route engine and swap runner in your browser above; your dollars' path below: your EVM or Solana wallet, deposit to NEAR Intents or THORChain, BCH to your swap contract (a BCH swap ends in your wallet), which Hashi trades on Cauldron with the swap's key for PUSD or MUSD in your own wallet; Reown AppKit, the Hashi relayer and keeper, Electrum servers and WizardConnect beneath" caption="You approve what leaves your wallets; the swap contract pays you at least the floor or sends the BCH back." />

1. **Every route, priced end to end.** NEAR Intents and THORChain are asked side by side, with Cauldron for the PUSD or MUSD leg, and ranked on what you keep after every fee and the gas to start.
2. **Your own two wallets.** An EVM or Solana wallet through Reown AppKit (WalletConnect), and a Bitcoin Cash wallet through WizardConnect. Each approves its own steps.
3. **One tracker across both chains.** Every step is saved in your browser, so a closed tab picks up where it stopped. Into PUSD or MUSD, the automatic trade runs while the tracker is open: come back to it, or the BCH goes back to your wallet about two hours after the start.

## The landing page

[hashi.cash](https://hashi.cash) opens on the story and live numbers, rebuilt at most once a minute: PUSD and MUSD liquidity and the last 24 hours of PUSD trading on Cauldron, the BCH price, which bridges are open, and a real 100 USDC → PUSD quote. Its **Open the app** leads to the swap screen.

<Callout title="No account, no Hashi fee">
Hashi has no sign-up and adds no fee of its own: what a route shows is what NEAR Intents, THORChain, Cauldron and the networks charge. It never holds your money.
</Callout>
