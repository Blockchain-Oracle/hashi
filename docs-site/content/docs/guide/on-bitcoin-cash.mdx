Pick two Bitcoin Cash tokens and the swap never leaves Bitcoin Cash: one Cauldron trade, built from your own coins, signed once in your Bitcoin Cash wallet. Only that wallet is needed.

| Pair | How it is built | Fees |
|---|---|---|
| BCH → PUSD or MUSD | the amount is everything that leaves your wallet; the pools get what remains after the fees | Cauldron 0.1% and the miner fee, from the BCH |
| PUSD or MUSD → BCH | your tokens are sold; the fees come out of the proceeds | Cauldron 0.1% and the miner fee, from the proceeds |
| PUSD ⇄ MUSD | one trade through both tokens' pools | Cauldron 0.1% in the sold token, **on top**; the miner fee from a little plain BCH in your wallet |

For PUSD ⇄ MUSD keep a little plain BCH in the wallet: about 0.00014 BCH for a trade across 54 pools. If it is short, the button says **Needs … BCH for the network fee**. **Max** leaves room for the 0.1% on top.

**You get at least** is enforced: the trade is built with that floor, and it settles at the built price or not at all.
