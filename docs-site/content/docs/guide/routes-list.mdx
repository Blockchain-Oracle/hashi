## What a row says

- **The legs, in order.** "NEAR Intents → Cauldron" going in, "Cauldron → THORChain" coming out, "Cauldron · no bridge" on Bitcoin Cash.
- **What arrives**, after the bridge's fee, Cauldron's fee, the miner fee and the price impact.
- **Time** (~3 min), **fees in dollars** (gas to start included) and **approvals**.
- **Best return** goes to what you keep after gas; **Fastest** appears only when a different route is quicker.

The best return is preselected; tap any row to take it. The ring by **Live** drains over 15 seconds, then the quotes refresh.

## When a route can't run

| Pill | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Paused** | the bridge has halted trading on a chain on this route | "THORChain has paused Base trading" |
| **Too small** | below the bridge's minimum, or too small for the network fees | "Below THORChain’s minimum for this route" |
| **Not offered** | the bridge has no pool for this token | "THORChain has no USDC pool on Solana" |
| **Low liquidity** | the price would move past your slippage, or Cauldron's pools can't fill the amount | "The price would move more than your slippage allows"; "Not enough Cauldron liquidity for this amount" |
| **Unavailable** | the provider answered with an error | – |

When a bridge reports its minimum, the row offers it in one tap: **Use 0.8124 USDC** in the capture below (THORChain's minimum). If every route is too small, the big button offers the smallest minimum too.
