Into Bitcoin Cash, the USDC or USDT can come from anywhere that sends tokens: an exchange withdrawal, a hardware wallet, a wallet with no WalletConnect. Nothing to connect on that side. A wallet that connects (a browser extension, a phone wallet) is still the quickest way: one approval.

## How it works

<Steps>
<Step>
On the swap card, switch **Connect wallet** to **Send from exchange**, and paste **your wallet, for refunds**: one of yours, on the chain you pay from (the ⓘ beside it says why).
</Step>
<Step>
**Review swap**, then **Start swap**. NEAR Intents gives this swap its own deposit address.
</Step>
<Step>
Send the amount shown, **on the network shown**. From an exchange, paste the address and the amount into its withdrawal form. From a phone wallet, scan the QR: it carries the coin, the network and the amount.
</Step>
<Step>
Hashi checks NEAR Intents every 5 seconds. Once the deposit is seen, the swap runs like any other: into BCH, or on through the swap contract into PUSD or MUSD.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Before you send

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Only the coin and network shown | The address takes that coin on that network. The same address on another network, or another coin, does not reach the swap. |
| At least the minimum shown | Anything from the minimum up is swapped (NEAR Intents' flexible amount), so an exchange's withdrawal fee is fine. Less than the minimum is refunded. |
| Within the countdown | The price was set for an hour. NEAR Intents keeps the address open for 72 hours, but a deposit after the hour comes back as a refund if the price has moved past your slippage. |
| A refund address of your own | A refund goes back on the chain you paid from. An exchange may not credit a refund sent to its address. |

## Coming out of Bitcoin Cash

The other way needs no wallet on the other chain either: switch **Connect wallet** to **Send to exchange** and paste your exchange's deposit address for that coin and network (any wallet address works too). NEAR Intents pays out with a plain token transfer from an ordinary account (checked on a real payout on Base), which an exchange credits like any withdrawal. Your Bitcoin Cash wallet still approves the sale.

## Where it works

Every swap into Bitcoin Cash through NEAR Intents, from all eight chains: Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche, BNB Chain and Solana. THORChain shows as **Not offered** in this mode: a token deposit there goes through its router contract, which a plain transfer cannot call. The Bitcoin Cash wallet stays connected: WizardConnect gives Hashi the address the BCH, PUSD or MUSD goes to.
