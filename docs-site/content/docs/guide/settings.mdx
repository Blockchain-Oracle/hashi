## Max slippage

**0.5%** · **Auto** (1%, the default) · **2%** · **3%** · **Other** (0.01% to 50%). "If the price moves more than this before your swap fills, the provider refunds you instead." Above 3% it warns: "High slippage: you may receive noticeably less." Your choice is kept in this browser.

## Send to another address

Allowed for **plain BCH into Bitcoin Cash** and for **every swap out of Bitcoin Cash**. PUSD and MUSD always land in your connected Bitcoin Cash wallet, because that wallet signs the Cauldron swap; a swap on Bitcoin Cash lands in the wallet that signs it too.

Hashi checks the address as you type:

| Side | Refused |
|---|---|
| EVM | a Bitcoin Cash address, an invalid address, a token contract ("Funds sent there are lost."), the zero address |
| Solana | an invalid address, a token mint |
| Bitcoin Cash | an EVM address, mixed upper and lower case, a typo (checksum), a testnet address |
