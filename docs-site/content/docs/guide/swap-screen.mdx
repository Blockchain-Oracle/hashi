## You pay

A big amount field (digits and one decimal point; a comma becomes a point), the token button with its chain badge, the amount in dollars, and once a wallet is connected your **balance** with **Max**. While the field is empty, presets fill it: **10 · 50 · 100** for dollar tokens, **0.01 · 0.05 · 0.1** for BCH.

**Max** is the whole balance, with two exceptions: BCH sent to a bridge keeps back 300 sats for the network fee, and PUSD ⇄ MUSD keeps back Cauldron's 0.1%, which is paid in the sold token on top.

## The flip

The round button between the panels turns the swap around and carries the selected route's received amount into **You pay**. It also clears a custom recipient and your route choice.

## You receive

The selected route's amount after every fee, which rolls when a fresh quote lands every 15 seconds and dims while it updates. Beside it, the dollar value and where it lands: your wallet's short address, a custom address, or a **Connect BCH wallet** chip.

## The big button

It always shows the first thing that is missing:

| Label | When |
|---|---|
| **Enter an amount** | the amount is empty |
| **Connect an EVM wallet** / **Connect a Solana wallet** | paying USDC or USDT, or receiving it with no custom address |
| **Connect a Bitcoin Cash wallet** | anything on the Bitcoin Cash side, unless plain BCH goes to a custom address |
| **Check the recipient address** | the custom address is not valid |
| **Not enough USDC** | the amount is above your balance |
| **Finding routes…** | quotes are on their way |
| **Too small. Use 0.15 USDC** | the amount is under every route's minimum; one tap fills in the smallest one |
| the reason, e.g. **No route for this pair right now** | no route can run |
| **Needs 0.00005 BCH for the network fee** | PUSD ⇄ MUSD with too little plain BCH |
| **Not enough ETH for gas on Base** | the paying chain's gas coin is short |
| **Review swap** | everything is ready |

## The header

**Swap**, the service status dot (**Live**, **Partly paused**, **Down**) and the settings gear, which turns vermilion when your slippage is not the default 1%. The moon or sun in the top bar switches light and dark; until you pick, Hashi follows your device.

|---|---|---|
