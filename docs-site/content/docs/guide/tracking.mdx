The tracker opens when you press **Start swap** and runs the swap itself: it asks your wallet for the deposit, checks the bridge every 5 seconds, and asks for the Cauldron step when the BCH arrives.

## What it shows

| Part | Words |
|---|---|
| Headline | **Approve the deposit** · **Send the BCH to NEAR Intents** (after a PUSD or MUSD sale) · **Bridging into Bitcoin Cash** · **Turning the BCH into PUSD** (the swap contract, no approval) · **One approval left** (after the BCH went back to your wallet) · **Swap complete** · **Refunded** · **The swap failed** · **Quote expired** |
| Under it | whether you can close the tab, e.g. "Safe to close this tab: the bridge delivers on its own." |
| Amounts | "You paid" and "You receive ≈ …" (the quote), then "You received …" (what arrived) |
| The one action | **Approve the deposit in your Base wallet**, **Swap the BCH into PUSD**, or "Checking with NEAR Intents every few seconds…" |
| **/ Across both chains** | each step with its chain, its time or the time left ("~2 min", "any moment"), and **View transaction** |
| **/ What happened** | every event, with its time |
| **Details for support** | the deposit address, THORChain memo, addresses, swap id and NEAR quote id |

## The steps

| Into Bitcoin Cash | Out of Bitcoin Cash |
|---|---|
| Deposit 50 USDC | Sell 20 PUSD on Cauldron (PUSD and MUSD only) |
| NEAR Intents turns it into BCH | Send the BCH to THORChain |
| BCH arrives in your wallet | THORChain turns it into USDC |
| Swap into PUSD on Cauldron (PUSD and MUSD only) | USDC on Ethereum arrives |

Explorer links go to the chain's own explorer, the NEAR Intents explorer for the deposit address, and thorchain.net for THORChain's inbound transaction.

## Taking longer than usual

Past one and a half times the estimate, the tracker says why: going in, the bridge waits for confirmations on the paying chain and refunds on its own if it cannot fill; coming out, Bitcoin Cash blocks come every 10 minutes on average, sometimes 20 or more, and the bridge starts once it counts the deposit as confirmed, which can take a few blocks. The tracker shows when NEAR Intents would start refunding a swap it has not finished.

## The finish

A finished swap plays a short chime and a buzz on phones that support it (when the swap was started in that same visit), and shows **Done in N min** with **Swap again**. A refund, failure or expired quote turns the stopped step red, gives the reason when there is one, and offers **Try again with a new quote**. If prices move past a floor mid-swap, the tracker offers **Continue at the new price** instead of asking your wallet ([When something goes wrong](/docs/routes/refunds)).
