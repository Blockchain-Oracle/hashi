The tracker opens when you press **Start swap** and runs the swap itself: it asks your wallet for the deposit, checks the bridge every 5 seconds, and, into PUSD or MUSD, makes the Cauldron trade from the swap contract when the BCH lands.

<Diagram name="into-bch" alt="Sequence for 50 USDC on Ethereum to PUSD through NEAR Intents and Cauldron in one approval: NEAR Intents pays the BCH to a contract made for the swap, and Hashi trades it with the swap's own key" caption="Into Bitcoin Cash, step by step: the deposit is the only approval." />

## What it shows

| Part | Words |
|---|---|
| Headline | **Approve the deposit** · **Send the BCH to THORChain** (after a PUSD or MUSD sale through THORChain) · **Bridging into Bitcoin Cash** · **Turning the BCH into PUSD** (the swap contract, no approval) · **One approval left** (after the BCH went back to your wallet) · **Swap complete** · **Refunded** · **The swap failed** · **Quote expired** |
| Under it | whether you can close the tab, e.g. "Safe to close this tab: the bridge delivers on its own." |
| Amounts | "You paid" and "You receive ≈ …" (the quote), then "You received …" (what arrived) |
| The one action | **Approve the deposit in your Base wallet** or **Swap the BCH into PUSD**; while a bridge carries the swap, the **/ Now** panel ([below](#while-the-bridge-works)) |
| **/ Across both chains** | each step with its chain, its time or what it waits for now ("waiting for block 2 of 2", "1:19 left"), and **View transaction** |
| **/ What happened** | every event, with its time |
| **Details for support** | the deposit address, THORChain memo, addresses, swap id and NEAR quote id |

## The steps

| Into Bitcoin Cash | Out of Bitcoin Cash |
|---|---|
| Deposit 50 USDC | Sell 20 PUSD on Cauldron (PUSD and MUSD only) |
| NEAR Intents turns it into BCH | Send the BCH to THORChain |
| BCH arrives at your swap contract (plain BCH: your wallet) | THORChain turns it into USDC |
| Swap into PUSD on Cauldron (PUSD and MUSD only) | USDC on Ethereum arrives |

Explorer links go to the chain's own explorer, the NEAR Intents explorer for the deposit address, and thorchain.net for THORChain's inbound transaction.

## While the bridge works

The **/ Now** panel says who has the swap and what they wait for, read live from the chain and the bridge every few seconds. A countdown appears only where the bridge gives an estimate; past it, the number counts up in amber.

| Moment | The panel says | The number |
|---|---|---|
| Coming out, waiting for blocks | **Waiting for block 2 of 2**, with Block 1 · Block 2 · NEAR swap filling in | time since the last block (blocks come at random, about every 10 minutes) |
| Both blocks in | **NEAR Intents is taking the deposit** | time since the second block |
| NEAR swapping | **NEAR Intents is swapping into USDC** | NEAR's own estimate counting down ("1:19 left"), then "+0:11 over" |
| Going in | **NEAR Intents is seeing your deposit on Base** | time waited |
| THORChain | its stages: looking for the deposit, waiting for blocks, swapping, sending | THORChain's own confirmation and payout timers |

Through NEAR Intents the panel also names the refund time: "Not done by 23:04? NEAR Intents sends the BCH back to your wallet." NEAR takes a Bitcoin Cash deposit after two blocks: on a real 0.48 PUSD sale (18 Nov 2026) it started 10 minutes after the second block and paid 0.476685 USDC on Base 6 minutes later.

If Hashi cannot read the bridge's status, the tracker says so plainly and links the bridge's own explorer; when nothing can be read at all, it falls back to the quoted estimate and explains what is slow.

## The finish

A finished swap plays a short chime and a buzz on phones that support it (when the swap was started in that same visit), and shows **Done in N min** with **Swap again**. A refund, failure or expired quote turns the stopped step red, gives the reason when there is one, and offers **Try again with a new quote**. If prices move past a floor mid-swap, nothing trades below it ([When something goes wrong](/docs/routes/refunds)).
