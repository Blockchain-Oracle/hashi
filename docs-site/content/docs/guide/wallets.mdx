## Your EVM or Solana wallet

Through **Reown AppKit**: browser wallets and WalletConnect wallets (MetaMask, Phantom, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet and hundreds more connect through it), on Base, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon, Avalanche, BNB Chain and Solana. Email and social logins, AppKit's own swaps, on-ramp and analytics are switched off; the modal follows Hashi's light or dark look.

No wallet to connect, or the USDC sits on an exchange? [Send from an exchange or wallet](/docs/guide/send-it-yourself): Hashi shows where to send it and watches for it.

## Your Bitcoin Cash wallet

Through **WizardConnect**, the Bitcoin Cash wallet protocol, which these wallets connect through: Selene (iPhone, Android), Paytaca (phones, browser), Cashonize (web, desktop) and Electron Cash (desktop plugin). HD wallets only.

On a phone, Open wallet app does it in one tap." />

1. Open your Bitcoin Cash wallet.
2. Scan the code, or paste the link (on a phone: **Open wallet app**).
3. Approve Hashi in the wallet.

The session stays until you disconnect, so a returning visit reconnects without scanning: the page reads **Reconnecting to your wallet…**, then **Reading your wallet…**, and never asks you to connect again. Your wallet holds its keys and approves every transaction that spends from it; Hashi only asks.

## When the wallet stops answering

A connected wallet answers Hashi every 10 seconds. If it stays silent for 25 seconds while Hashi is on screen (the wallet app closed, the phone asleep, or Hashi removed in the wallet), the wallets menu and the swap's page say **Not answering** with **Pair again**: it drops the old session and shows a fresh code. A wallet that ends the session itself is announced the moment it does.

## The wallets menu

**Connect** in the top bar (or **Wallets** once connected) lists **EVM wallet**, **Solana wallet** and your Bitcoin Cash wallet, each with its short address, a copy button and **Disconnect**.
