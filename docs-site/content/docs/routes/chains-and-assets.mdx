## Every pair

14 stablecoins × 3 Bitcoin Cash tokens × 2 directions = **84 bridged pairs**: NEAR Intents carries all 84, THORChain 42. Plus **6 pairs on Bitcoin Cash** (PUSD, MUSD and BCH with each other) on Cauldron alone.
