Neither NEAR Intents nor THORChain lists PUSD or MUSD: they move plain BCH, and PUSD and MUSD trade only on Cauldron. So every route Hashi shows is one of these:

| Route | Into Bitcoin Cash | Out of Bitcoin Cash |
|---|---|---|
| **NEAR Intents** (+ Cauldron for PUSD, MUSD) | all 14 stablecoins | all 14 stablecoins |
| **THORChain** (+ Cauldron for PUSD, MUSD) | 7: USDC Base; USDC and USDT on Ethereum, Avalanche, BNB Chain | the same 7 |
| **Cauldron alone** | PUSD ⇄ MUSD ⇄ BCH, no bridge | |

## Out of Bitcoin Cash

<OutFigure />

Selling PUSD or MUSD through NEAR Intents takes **one approval**: the Cauldron sale pays NEAR's deposit address in the same transaction. NEAR's quote takes any deposit from its minimum up, and Hashi checks the built sale pays at least that minimum, and that NEAR still pays what the review promised, before asking you to sign. Through THORChain it takes two (its deposit needs its own memo): the sale (held to your slippage, and saved), then the BCH, with THORChain quoted at that moment for exactly what the sale produced.
