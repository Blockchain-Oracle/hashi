NEAR Intents moves value across chains through a network of solvers. Hashi uses its **1Click** API (`1click.chaindefuser.com`) for prices, deposit addresses and status straight from your browser. Quotes go through Hashi's server, which adds Hashi's partner key: they follow NEAR Intents' partner fee schedule instead of the 0.25% that unauthenticated quotes pay (on 21 Nov 2026 a 1 USDC to BCH quote paid out 0.13% more), and the key never reaches your browser. It carries all 14 stablecoins on all eight chains, to and from BCH. Every swap carries Hashi's referral, and every one of them, from anyone, is listed on [the Explorer](https://hashi.cash/explorer).

| Hashi asks | When |
|---|---|
| `GET /v0/tokens` | the token list and USD prices (BCH, the gas coins) |
| `POST /v0/quote` (dry) | every route price, refreshed every 15 seconds |
| `POST /v0/quote` (real) | **Start swap**: issues a deposit address for this swap |
| `POST /v0/deposit/submit` | right after your deposit, to point the solvers at it |
| `GET /v0/status` | every 5 seconds until the swap settles |

## How a swap runs

1. **Start swap** asks for a real quote (selling PUSD or MUSD, a quote that takes any deposit from its minimum up, so the Cauldron sale can pay it directly); NEAR Intents returns a **deposit address** that belongs to this swap alone. Hashi keeps the signed quote on the swap, as NEAR asks integrators to.
2. Your wallet sends the exact amount to that address: one transfer on an EVM chain or Solana, or one Bitcoin Cash payment coming out.
3. Solvers fill it. Going in, the BCH arrives at your plain Bitcoin Cash address, or, for PUSD and MUSD, at the swap's contract (NEAR doesn't accept token-aware `z…` addresses, so PUSD and MUSD come from the Cauldron trade).

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Swap type | exact input; selling PUSD or MUSD, flexible input (any deposit from its minimum up) |
| Deadline | 1 hour going in; 4 hours coming out (NEAR counts a Bitcoin Cash deposit after it confirms, which can take a few blocks). A swap NEAR has not finished by then is refunded to your wallet |
| Refunds go to | your paying address: the stablecoin wallet going in, your plain BCH address coming out |
| Minimum | read from NEAR's answer and offered as **Use …** in one tap |
| Hashi's fee | none (the quote carries a `hashi` referral label, no fee) |

Status mapping: **PROCESSING** → bridging; **SUCCESS** → done (or "BCH arrived" when PUSD or MUSD is next); **REFUNDED** → refunded, with NEAR's reason; **FAILED** → failed.
