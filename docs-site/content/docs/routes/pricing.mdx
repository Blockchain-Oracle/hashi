## The fee lines

Every line is in dollars, from NEAR Intents' live prices. Hashi never assumes a stablecoin is worth exactly $1.

| Line | Into Bitcoin Cash | Out of Bitcoin Cash | On Bitcoin Cash |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The bridge** | what you pay, minus the BCH that crossed (fee and spread together) | the BCH that crossed, minus what you receive | – |
| **Cauldron swap** | Router 0.1% + miner fee + price impact against the pre-trade price | the same, for the sale | the same; PUSD ⇄ MUSD adds the 0.1% in the sold token |
| **Network fee** | gas to deposit on the paying chain | the Bitcoin Cash send to the bridge (300 sats) | – |

The 1,000 sats a bought token carries, and any change, stay with you, so they are not counted.

## Gas, measured

| Route | Gas counted |
|---|---|
| NEAR Intents, EVM chains | one token transfer to a fresh address (measured per token, 51,900–80,200; Avalanche USDC copied from Base's identical contract) |
| THORChain, EVM chains | one allowance (46,500–56,400) + the deposit (71,000, the highest of four real deposits, rounded up) |
| Solana | the 5,000-lamport signature + rent for the deposit address's token account |

Gas units × the chain's live gas price × the gas coin's live price. If your wallet holds less than that, the button says **Not enough ETH for gas on Base** (or, once Hashi's relayer is funded, offers [gasless USDC](/docs/guide/gasless)).

## Ranking

Routes are sorted by **what you keep**: the amount out minus the gas you pay outside the quote, restated in the token you receive. Ties go to the faster route. **Best return** is the first; **Fastest** shows only when a different route is quicker.
