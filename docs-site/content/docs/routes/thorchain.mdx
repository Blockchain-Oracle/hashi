THORChain swaps native assets through its own pools. Hashi reads it through the Liquify gateway, from your browser.

| Hashi asks | For |
|---|---|
| `GET /quote/swap` | price, minimum, and on **Start swap** the vault, router, memo and expiry |
| `GET /inbound_addresses` | which chains are halted (every 30 seconds) |
| `GET /tx/status/{hash}` | the swap's progress, every 5 seconds |

## Coverage

USDC on Base; USDC and USDT on Ethereum, Avalanche and BNB Chain. Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Solana are NEAR Intents only ("Not offered" on THORChain's row).

## How Hashi uses it

- **Slippage** goes as `liquidity_tolerance_bps`, THORChain's preferred setting. The older `tolerance_bps` also counts the fixed outbound fee, so small swaps failed at 1%.
- **Halts**: if THORChain has halted or paused trading on the stablecoin's chain or on BCH, the route shows **Paused** with the chain's name.
- **Minimum**: THORChain's recommended minimum; below it the row says so and offers it in one tap.
- **Going in** (EVM): your wallet allows THORChain's router to move the exact amount (Hashi skips this when enough is already allowed), then calls `depositWithExpiry` with the memo.
- **Refunds**: going in, your stablecoin wallet is sent as the refund address, and it rides in the memo. Coming out, THORChain refuses a refund address in a Bitcoin Cash memo ("generated memo too long for source chain"), so it refunds to the address of the deposit's first coin, and Hashi puts a coin from your receive address first.
- **Addresses**: Bitcoin Cash addresses go without their `bitcoincash:` prefix, which THORChain's memo parser cannot read.
- **Coming out** (Bitcoin Cash): one payment built to THORChain's rules: your coin is input 0, the vault is output 0, the memo is an OP_RETURN of at most 80 bytes, change returns to input 0's address.

Status mapping: inbound seen → bridging; an outbound transaction → done (or "BCH arrived"); a planned refund → refunded.
