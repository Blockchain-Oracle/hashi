## Arrival estimates

| Route | Estimate |
|---|---|
| NEAR Intents, into Bitcoin Cash | NEAR's own estimate from your deposit, + 15 s for the Cauldron step |
| THORChain, into Bitcoin Cash | THORChain's swap time, + 15 s for the Cauldron step |
| NEAR Intents, out of Bitcoin Cash | two Bitcoin Cash blocks (about 20 min on average) + NEAR's estimate |
| THORChain, out of Bitcoin Cash | THORChain's swap time (10 min if it gives none), + 15 s when selling PUSD or MUSD |
| Cauldron only | 15 s |

Quoted estimates from live price quotes on 16 November 2026: 50 USDC on Ethereum → PUSD ~3 min through NEAR Intents; 20 PUSD → USDC on Ethereum ~10 min through THORChain; 10 PUSD → MUSD ~15 s on Cauldron.

## How long things last

| What | How long |
|---|---|
| A route's quote | fresh 10 s, refreshed every 15 s |
| A NEAR Intents deposit address | 1 hour going in, 4 hours coming out |
| A THORChain quote | until its expiry (THORChain sets it; going in, its router refuses later deposits) |
| A swap that starts with a Cauldron trade, unstarted | 1 hour, then it ends as **Expired** |
| A gasless authorisation | 30 minutes |
| The tracker's status checks | every 5 seconds while the bridge works |

## Minimums

Each bridge sets its own, and they change. When an amount is too small the row says **Too small** with the reason, and offers **Use …** with the bridge's real minimum. When no route can carry the amount, the big button offers the smallest minimum the same way, e.g. **Too small. Use 0.15 USDC**. Minimums are always shown in the token you pay with, rounded up to the decimals the screen shows. A Cauldron trade too small for its own network fees says "Too small to cover the Bitcoin Cash network fees".
